Material Design classic color palette

Source: Google, Material Design — Color / The color palette
https://m1.material.io/style/color.html#color-color-palette

AutoClicker includes all 19 color families with their published 50–900 and,
where available, A100/A200/A400/A700 shades, plus Black and White (256 colors).
RGB values are preserved from the official table. The application's theme
mappings, dark surfaces and text-contrast adaptations are AutoClicker choices,
not additional palettes published by Google.

The original data snapshot is tools/material_palette_source.json. Native
Android color/style resources and the Kotlin catalogue are generated by
tools/generate_material_palettes.py. No Material Components dependency or
runtime download is needed.
